Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 30, 2026
Last Updated: June 30, 2026

MindAware is developed and operated by ChiCode ("we," "us," or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use the MindAware mobile application ("the App"), how we use and protect that information, and your rights regarding it. By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of information:

Account Information

  • Email address, provided when you create an account or sign in via Supabase Authentication.
  • Display name or username, if you choose to set one.

Usage & Progress Data

  • Session data: focus durations, presence scores, deep-focus metrics, and session timestamps.
  • Game data: Opal balances, Zen Leaves earned, daily streak counts, and co-op session history.
  • Forest data: trees grown, Legacy Forest entries, and session milestones.
  • App interaction events used to improve features and fix bugs.

Device & Technical Information

  • Device type, operating system version, and app version.
  • Crash logs and diagnostic data to identify and resolve technical issues.

Subscription & Purchase Data

  • Your subscription status (free, trial, or premium), managed through RevenueCat.
  • We do not receive or store your payment card details — all payments are processed directly by the Apple App Store or Google Play Store.

Advertising Data (Free Users Only)

  • If you use the free version of MindAware, Google AdMob may collect your advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, GAID on Android) and device information to serve contextually relevant ads.
  • On iOS, AdMob advertising tracking requires your explicit permission under Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework. You will be prompted before any tracking occurs.
  • Premium subscribers do not see ads and are not subject to AdMob data collection.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect solely to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain the App and its features.
  • Sync your progress, forest, and session history across your devices.
  • Manage and verify your subscription status and entitlements.
  • Display relevant advertisements to free users via Google AdMob.
  • Send you important notices about the App, your account, or your subscription (e.g., trial expiry, billing issues).
  • Analyze aggregated, anonymized usage patterns to improve the App.
  • Detect, prevent, and address technical issues, abuse, or violations of our Terms.

We do not use your data for profiling, behavioral advertising beyond AdMob's standard ad serving, or any purpose unrelated to delivering the MindAware experience.

3. Third-Party Services

We work with the following third-party service providers. Each has its own privacy policy that governs how they handle data:

Supabase

Provides our database and authentication infrastructure. Your account credentials and session/progress data are stored on Supabase servers located in the United States. Supabase is SOC 2 Type II certified. Privacy policy: supabase.com/privacy.

RevenueCat

Manages subscription purchases, entitlements, and trial periods. RevenueCat receives your subscription status from the App Store or Google Play; we only receive the resulting entitlement (free, trial, or premium). Privacy policy: revenuecat.com/privacy.

Google AdMob

Serves advertisements in the free version of the App. AdMob may collect your advertising identifier, IP address, and device information. On iOS, this requires your ATT consent. On Android, you may opt out via your device's advertising settings. Privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We share data only:

  • With the third-party service providers listed above, to the extent necessary to deliver the App.
  • When required by law, court order, or valid government request.
  • To protect the rights, safety, or property of ChiCode, our users, or the public.
  • In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, in which case you will be notified.

5. Data Retention

We retain your account and progress data for as long as your account is active. If you request account deletion, we will permanently delete your personal data within 30 days, except where retention is required by law or for legitimate business purposes (e.g., resolving disputes or preventing fraud).

Aggregated, anonymized analytics data that cannot identify you may be retained indefinitely to improve the App.

6. Your Rights & Data Deletion

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Request deletion of your personal data ("right to be forgotten").
  • Object to or restrict certain processing of your data.
  • Withdraw consent at any time (where processing is based on consent).
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority (EU/EEA users).

To delete your account and all associated data, go to Profile → Settings → Delete Account within the App, or email us at chicodelabs@gmail.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request."

To opt out of personalized ads on iOS, go to Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking and disable tracking for MindAware. On Android, go to Settings → Google → Ads → Delete advertising ID.

7. Children's Privacy

MindAware is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will promptly delete it. If you believe a child under 13 has used the App and provided personal data, please contact us at chicodelabs@gmail.com.

Users between 13 and 17 should have a parent or guardian review and agree to this Privacy Policy before using the App.

8. International Data Transfers

MindAware is operated from the United States. If you access the App from outside the United States, your information may be transferred to and processed in the US and other countries where our service providers operate. These countries may have data protection laws that differ from those in your country.

Where required by law (e.g., for users in the European Economic Area), we rely on appropriate safeguards — such as Standard Contractual Clauses — for such transfers.

9. California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, you have the right under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) to:

  • Know what personal information we collect, use, disclose, and sell (we do not sell personal information).
  • Request deletion of your personal information.
  • Opt out of the sale of personal information (not applicable — we do not sell data).
  • Non-discrimination for exercising your CCPA rights.

To exercise these rights, email chicodelabs@gmail.com with "CCPA Request" in the subject line.

10. App Tracking Transparency (iOS)

On iOS 14.5 and later, if you are a free user, MindAware will request permission to track your activity across apps and websites using Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework. This permission is used solely to enable Google AdMob to serve relevant ads. You may deny this request at any time; ads will still be shown but will be less personalized. Premium subscribers are never shown ads and will not be asked for tracking permission.

11. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data, including encrypted data transmission (HTTPS/TLS), secure authentication via Supabase, and access controls limiting who can view user data. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. We encourage you to use a strong, unique password and to notify us immediately of any suspected unauthorized access.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make material changes, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, notify you in-app or by email. Your continued use of the App after changes take effect constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
